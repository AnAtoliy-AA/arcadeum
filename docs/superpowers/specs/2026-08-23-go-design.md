# Go — Design Spec (ARC-887)

## Overview

Classic Go (Baduk/Weiqi) for Arcadeum. Two players alternate placing stones;
groups with no liberties are captured; two consecutive passes end the game and
Chinese area scoring decides the winner.

- **gameId**: `go_v1` · **Widget**: `GoGame` · **Landing**: `/games/go`
- **Players**: 2 (black & white). Black moves first; colors assigned randomly.
- **Boards**: 9×9, 13×13, 19×19 (`boardSize` option).
- **Category**: Board Game · **Start mode**: immediate.

## Rules implemented

1. Stones placed on empty intersections only.
2. After placement, opposing groups with zero liberties are captured.
3. Suicide is illegal (placement that leaves own group without liberties and
   captures nothing).
4. Simple ko: immediately recapturing at a single-stone-capture point is
   forbidden (`koPoint` in state, cleared on any other action).
5. `pass_turn` always available while playing. Two consecutive passes → game
   over, board scored.
6. Scoring — **Chinese area**: score = stones on board + empty regions bordered
   exclusively by that color (+ komi 7.5 for white). Higher score wins; draws
   impossible with fractional komi but `getResult` handles them anyway.

## Bot strategy

- **easy** — uniform random legal move (never fills a true eye), never
  misses an immediate capture.
- **medium** — greedy heuristic: capture > escape/save atari > atari opponent >
  big liberties gain, mild positional bias toward star points early, avoids
  self-atari and filling own eyes.
- **hard** — UCT MCTS, ≤ 220 simulations and a 500 ms wall-clock budget.
- **expert** — UCT MCTS, ≤ 550 simulations and a 900 ms wall-clock budget.
- MCTS backpropagation flips the reward per ply (zero-sum), so every level of
  the tree models a hostile opponent.
- Playouts: locality-biased light-random with capture preference, evaluated via
  in-place probes (no board copies), never fill true eyes, capped length;
  terminal = double pass or cap. The time budget bounds event-loop blocking
  regardless of board size. Defensive pass fallback when no legal moves exist.
